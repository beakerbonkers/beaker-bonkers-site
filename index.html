About the scientist

Hi! I'm the kid behind Beaker Bonkers. I started this channel because I got a chemistry set for my birthday and, well... I never really stopped. Now I turn our kitchen into a lab every weekend and film whatever chaos happens next. (Sorry, Mom.)

My favorite thing in the whole world is that moment right before an experiment works — or explodes — or does something nobody expected. That's the best part of science: you genuinely don't always know what's going to happen.

Our mission: to show kids that science isn't a subject you sit through — it's a way of asking "what if?" and then actually finding out. Every experiment on this channel is something YOU can try, with a grown-up's help and stuff from around the house.
